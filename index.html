Three turnkey designs

10x here means ten specialized roles, not a promised return. Click a card. The gate below records your paper approval and refuses live routing.

Design A · Relay

Signal relay

TradingView alert posts JSON to the desk. One bot checks symbol, size cap, and spread. You approve. Coinbase preview or Kraken read-only quote returns. No order without the second click.

Design B · Ensemble

Ten-role book

Scanner, regime, signal, risk, sizer, venue router, execution, exit, treasury, audit. CryptoHopper may feed the signal role. Treasury never holds a withdraw key. Audit writes every reject.

Design C · Mirror

Hopper mirror

CryptoHopper keeps the exchange connection you already trust. Lynx only mirrors the proposal, scores it, and asks you. Phantom and Kraken Wallet are cold addresses, displayed, never signed.

Human gate

None selected

Paper mode records the choice on this browser. Live mode is hard-disabled. Withdrawal, Transfer, and Deposit permissions are out of scope on every venue.
